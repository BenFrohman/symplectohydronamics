This page is the public host for the last six months of the Frohmanian program: the Lean 4 formalization of the symplectic tether, the living Navier–Stokes manuscripts, and the branches that grew out of that geometry — combinatorial game theory, fusion/MHD control, neural scaling, and a coordination fabric for frontier training fleets.

Honest scope. The Lean corpus and tether manuscripts are a priority-preserving formalization and geometric architecture. They contain novel definitions, schematic skeletons, and remaining analytic work. They do not claim a completed, kernel-closed solution of the Clay Navier–Stokes Millennium Problem. Read the GitHub README on the Lean repo before citing a “proof.”
