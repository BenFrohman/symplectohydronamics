SYMPLECTIC TETHER · FILMS

Three 1080p films rendered from closed-form exact solutions of the incompressible Navier–Stokes equations on the periodic torus T³ = [0,2π)³. Every frame shows 15,625 particles moved with RK4 and 40 vortex lines (integral curves of ω). The on-screen HUD recomputes energy E, enstrophy Z and helicity H on a 24³ grid and shows them next to the exact values. It also shows the residual of the energy identity dE/dt + 2νZ. Across all frames of all three films, the largest relative residual is about 4.5×10⁻⁶ and max|∇·u| ≤ 4×10⁻¹⁵.

These are exact special solutions. They demonstrate the energy identity for these explicit smooth flows only, and they say nothing about general 3D regularity or the Navier–Stokes Millennium Problem. The camera paths, bloom and colour maps are cinematography. Renderer, diagnostics and reproduction steps: github.com/BenFrohman/symplectic-tether-sim/films.
